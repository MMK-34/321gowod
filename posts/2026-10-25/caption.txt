Sunday. 16 minutes. Anywhere. No excuses left. 🌤️

No gym, no gear, no problem. Put a timer on your phone, clear a square of floor and give it sixteen honest minutes. Then go enjoy your Sunday.

━━━━━━━━━━━━
SUNDAY EIGHTS · TABATA
8 × (20 s work / 10 s rest) each · 1 min rest between:
▪️ 8× Jump Squats
▪️ 8× Push-ups
▪️ 8× Hollow Rocks
▪️ 8× Burpees
━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚖️ Scaled: Air squats · knee push-ups · tuck hold · step-back burpees
🏠 No gear: Already zero gear — living room, park, hotel room.
🎯 Target: Sum of your lowest rounds

💡 Coach tip: Count every round and write down the lowest number for each movement. Your score is the sum — so don't blow up in round one.
