No pacing decisions today. The clock is your coach. ⏱️

Four stations, sixteen minutes, zero thinking. EMOMs are perfect when your head is busy — just show up for every minute and the work takes care of itself.

━━━━━━━━━━━━
CLOCKWORK · EMOM 16
Every minute on the minute, 4 rounds:
▪️ Min 1 12 Wall Balls (9 / 6 kg)
▪️ Min 2 10 Toes-to-Bar
▪️ Min 3 12 / 10 Cal Row (♂ / ♀)
▪️ Min 4 8 DB Snatches (22.5 / 15 kg · alternating)
━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚖️ Scaled: 10 wall balls 6/4 kg · knee raises · 10/8 cal · DB 15/10 kg
🏠 No gear: 15 air squats · 12 V-ups · 10 burpees · 20 lunges
🎯 Target: 15–20 s rest every minute

💡 Coach tip: If you finish with less than 10 seconds of rest, drop 2 reps next round. Quality beats survival.
