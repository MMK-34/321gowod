Sunday reset. 16 minutes, one square metre of floor, zero excuses. 🌿

After a big week, Sunday is about moving well, not moving to exhaustion. Hop side to side, climb, bridge and hold — your body will thank you on Monday.

━━━━━━━━━━━━
RESET BUTTON · TABATA
8 × (20 s work / 10 s rest) each · 1 min rest between:
▪️ 8× Skater Hops
▪️ 8× Mountain Climbers
▪️ 8× Glute Bridges
▪️ 8× Plank Shoulder Taps
━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚖️ Scaled: Skater steps · slow climbers · knee shoulder taps
🏠 No gear: Already zero gear — living room, park, hotel room.
🎯 Target: Same reps in round 1 and round 8

💡 Coach tip: Don't sprint the first 20 seconds. Pick a pace you can repeat eight times — that's the whole game.
