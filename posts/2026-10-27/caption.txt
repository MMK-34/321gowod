A steady back-and-forth Tuesday. Every minute asks one clear question — and gives you a little rest if you answer it fast. Great day to work on smooth, efficient reps instead of fast, messy ones.

PENDULUM · EMOM 16
Every minute on the minute, 4 rounds:
▪️ Min 1 12 DB Snatches (22.5 / 15 kg · alternating)
▪️ Min 2 10 Pull-ups
▪️ Min 3 10 Box Jumps (60 / 50 cm)
▪️ Min 4 15 Sit-ups
━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚖️ Scaled: DB 15/10 kg · ring rows · box step-ups · 12 sit-ups
🏠 No gear: Jumping lunges · supermans · tuck jumps · sit-ups
🎯 Target: 15–25 s rest every minute

💡 Coach tip: Pull-ups: pick your strategy before the clock starts. If 10 unbroken isn't a sure thing, do 5 + 5 from round one — quick sets beat failed reps.
