Day 1. Three rounds. Nowhere to hide. 🔥

A short, honest Monday opener. Run, swing, pull — nothing fancy, just enough to wake up the whole body and set the tone for the week.

━━━━━━━━━━━━
IGNITION · FOR TIME
3 rounds for time of:
▪️ 400 m Run
▪️ 21 Kettlebell Swings (24 / 16 kg)
▪️ 12 Pull-ups
━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚖️ Scaled: 300 m run · KB 16/12 kg · ring rows instead of pull-ups
🏠 No gear: Jump squats instead of swings · burpees instead of pull-ups
🎯 Target: 9–13 min

💡 Coach tip: Treat the 400 m as a strong jog, not a sprint. You win this workout by going straight into the swings without standing around.
