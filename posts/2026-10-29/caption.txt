Press. Burpee. Add three. Find out where your shoulders give up. 🪜

The first rungs fly by and feel almost too easy. Then the bar gets heavier every time you pick it up, and the burpees stop being a rest. Somewhere around 12 reps the real workout starts.

━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRESSURE POINT · LADDER
3-6-9-12… — add 3 reps every round, 12 min:
▪️ +3 Push Press (50 / 35 kg)
▪️ +3 Bar-facing Burpees
━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚖️ Scaled: Push press 35/25 kg · step-back burpees
🏠 No gear: Pike push-ups + burpees, same ladder
🎯 Target: Reach the 15-rep rung

💡 Coach tip: Dip and drive with your legs — the push press is a leg movement that ends overhead. Shoulders alone won't get you to 15.
