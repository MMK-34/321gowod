Easy start. Then it isn't. How high can you climb? 🪜

The first rounds feel like a warm-up — that's the trap. Every rung adds two reps, and somewhere around 10 the ladder starts asking real questions.

━━━━━━━━━━━━
STAIRWAY · LADDER
2-4-6-8-10… — add 2 reps every round, 12 min:
▪️ +2 Deadlifts (80 / 55 kg)
▪️ +2 Box Jump-overs (60 / 50 cm)
━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚖️ Scaled: Deadlifts 60/40 kg · box step-overs
🏠 No gear: Push-ups + squat jumps, same ladder
🎯 Target: Reach the 12-rep rung

💡 Coach tip: Keep the deadlifts touch-and-go only while your back stays flat. The moment it rounds, switch to quick singles.
