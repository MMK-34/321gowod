Mid-week AMRAP. The goal isn't to start fast — it's to finish strong. 🧠

A barbell, the floor and a short run. Simple pieces, but twenty minutes of them teaches you more about pacing than any long explanation could. Swipe for the scaling options and the game plan →

━━━━━━━━━━━━
LONG GAME · AMRAP 20
As many rounds as possible in 20 min:
▪️ 5 Power Cleans (60 / 40 kg)
▪️ 10 Push-ups
▪️ 15 Air Squats
▪️ 200 m Run
━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚖️ Scaled: Cleans 40/30 kg · incline push-ups
🏠 No gear: 10 jumping lunges instead of cleans
🎯 Target: 6–9 rounds

💡 Coach tip: Write your round count on the wall after every lap. Seeing the number keeps you honest.
