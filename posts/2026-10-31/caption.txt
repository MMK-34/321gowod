THE GRIND · CHIPPER
For time, one pass straight through:
▪️ 1000 m Row
▪️ 50 Goblet Squats (24 / 16 kg)
▪️ 40 Push-ups
▪️ 30 Burpee Box Jump-overs (60 / 50 cm)
▪️ 20 Chest-to-Bar Pull-ups
▪️ 1000 m Row
━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚖️ Scaled: 750 m rows · KB 16/12 kg · incline push-ups · step-overs
🏠 No gear: 1 km runs · air squats · burpee broad jumps · supermans
🎯 Target: 28–38 min

💡 Coach tip: Break everything early and on purpose. The people who finish fastest are the ones who never sit down.
